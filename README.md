# Sebastiano Marchesini 👋

**Solution Architect — SAP Business Technology Platform, S/4HANA, and agentic AI.**
📍 New York City · 🏢 [Avvale](https://www.avvale.com/partners/sap) (SAP Gold Partner)

⏳ I work on the part of enterprise IT that has a hard deadline. Thousands of U.S.
companies — manufacturers, food producers, hospitals, defense contractors — still
run their operations on SAP ECC, and mainstream maintenance ends in 2027. Migrating
each one is a multi-year, multi-million-dollar re-architecture, and there are not
enough practitioners who have done it.

So most of what I build and publish is aimed at making that migration **repeatable
instead of bespoke**: reusable BTP automation frameworks, reference architectures for
clean-core extensibility, and training material for the people who have to execute it.

---

## 🛠️ What I work on

☁️ **SAP BTP** — Cloud Foundry and Kyma runtimes, CAP services, multitenant extensions,
Neo → Cloud Foundry migration (the subject of my SAP PRESS book).

⚙️ **S/4HANA** — public and private cloud rollouts, clean-core extensibility, CDS-based
analytics, OAuth/principal propagation patterns for S/4HANA Public Cloud APIs.

🤖 **Agentic AI in ERP** — autonomous agents in SAP Joule Studio, MCP servers over CAP
services, LLM-assisted development workflows inside Business Application Studio and
VS Code.

🔄 **Intelligent automation** — architect of **iAPS**, a reusable BTP accounts-payable
automation framework deployed across multiple U.S. enterprises in regulated and
critical-infrastructure sectors.
